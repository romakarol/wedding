Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the weather like?
It will likely be in the high 20s °C.
Do I need an adapter?
Yes. Moldova uses type C and F plugs.
What’s the main language?
Romanian is the most common language. Russian and English are also spoken.
What’s the currency?
The Moldovan leu (MDL). The approximate exchange rate is €1 to 20 MDL.
How long is the flight?
Usually around 3.5 to 4 hours.
Is there a time zone difference?
Moldova is 2 hours ahead of Ireland. When it’s 6pm in Ireland, it’s 8pm in Moldova.
What is there to do in Orhei?
  • OrheiLand amusement park
  • Lake beach
  • Lakefront park
  • Ivanos Park
Will there be organised events?
We’ll share more details when they’re confirmed. Check back for updates!
